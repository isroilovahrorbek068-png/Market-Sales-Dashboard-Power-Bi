
# Sales Performance Dashboard 📊🛒

## 📌 Introduction

This project features a **Sales Performance Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze product profitability, sales volume, and product quality distribution.

The dashboard helps stakeholders quickly understand **which products generate the most profit**, how sales are distributed by quality, and how product count relates to overall revenue.

---


## 🧰 Tools Used

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Excel / CSV data sources**

---

## 🛠 Skills Showcased

### 🔹 Data Transformation (ETL)

* Cleaned and prepared raw sales data using Power Query
* Standardized product names and categories
* Created calculated columns for profit, quantity, and sales quality classification

### 🔹 DAX & Measures

* Created measures for:

  * Total Profit
  * Total Quantity Sold
  * Profit by Product
  * Product Count
  * Top 5 Products of the Month

### 🔹 Data Visualization

* KPI cards for Total Profit and Total Quantity
* Bar chart displaying Top 5 products of the month
* Pie chart showing Sales Quality Categories
* Combo chart (Column + Line) comparing total profit and product count

### 🔹 Dashboard Design & Storytelling

* Clean and simple layout for easy interpretation
* Clear KPI hierarchy to highlight key metrics
* Consistent color usage to improve readability

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard enables analysis of:

* Overall profit and sales quantity
* Most profitable and best-selling products
* Sales quality distribution (High, Medium, Low)
* Relationship between product variety and profitability

This supports **data-driven sales and inventory decisions**.

### 🥇 Top 5 Products of the Month

* **PC:** $7.0K
* **iPod:** $6.9K
* **Laptop‑HP:** $3.0K
* **Redmi Note 13 Pro:** $2.8K

➡ iPhone 15 is the highest profit‑generating product

---

### 🧾 Sales Quality Categories

* **High Quality (A’lo):** 55.88%
* **Medium Quality (O‘rta):** 39.71%
* **Low Quality (Yomon):** 4.41%

➡ Most sales come from high‑quality products

---

### 📦 Profit vs Product Count

* High‑value products (iPhone 15, iPod) generate more profit with fewer product variations
* Accessories (keyboard, mouse) show lower profitability

➡ Focusing on premium products increases overall profit
